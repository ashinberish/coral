Coral

A hydration app with game-style energy: chunky press-down buttons, streaks, daily quests, pearls and badges. A 3D Apple-style icon; flat, colorful screens; no water drops.

3D Apple-style icon

A seahorse with soft light, a slight lift and a glossy top, made like a real iOS icon. The icon is the only place with gradients.

Duolingo-style energy

Chunky buttons and cards with a solid bottom edge that presses down with a haptic. Rounded, bold type. Streaks, daily quests, pearls and badges.

Coral at the center

Coral is the main color (ring, buttons, tabs), with bright supporting colors for rewards. Its own look, not a copy of Duolingo.
