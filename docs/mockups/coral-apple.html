Coral

A calm hydration reminder in the style of Apple's own apps. Close your ring and keep your streak. One coral accent, system colors, no gradients, no mascot and no water drops.

One accent: coral

Coral is the only brand color, as Fitness uses pink and Notes uses yellow. Everything else uses iOS system colors, so light and dark mode adapt automatically.

A seahorse as a symbol, not a character

A single-color seahorse in the style of an SF Symbol. It appears only on the app icon and the welcome screen. No cartoon and no water drops.

Streak first

A streak chip on Today, a Streak tab with a calendar, freezes that protect a missed day, milestone badges and an evening "streak at risk" reminder.
